## Scope of this fork

This fork focuses on character animation through local ComfyUI and contains three skills:

| Skill | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `comfyui-forge-generate2dmedia` | Local ComfyUI image-to-video generation, workflow checks, and job resumption. |
| `comfyui-forge-generate2dsprite` | Character reference inspection, background removal, and input preparation. |
| `comfyui-forge-video2dsprite` | Video-to-transparent-animation conversion, frame selection, retiming, and delivery. |

Key changes from upstream include:

- Video generation exclusively through local ComfyUI, removing cloud APIs, API-key configuration, and CLI generation fallback routes.
- Independent skill names, directories, and installation records for coexistence with the original.
- User-workflow node bindings, configuration-path resolution, job resumption, and result verification.
- Specification planning, frame alignment, and project preferences, with confirmations and explanations in the user's language.
- Action-specific starting-image assessment, framing margins, consistent character scale, translation handling, motion review, and retry decisions.
- Selection of complete actions, playback retiming, preservation of key poses, and preview and acceptance at final asset dimensions.
- Standard ZIP delivery of transparent PNG frames, a transparent PNG sheet, a GIF preview, and the source MP4.
- Offline tests and Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, and Korean READMEs and guides for installation, workflow configuration, usage, changes, and examples.

This fork excludes upstream map generation, code-drawn art, standalone static sheet/icon processing, and additional engine transports and runtimes. Still-image generation is not configured. Users supply their own workflows and models.

ComfyUI, Python packages, FFmpeg, models, third-party nodes, and content separately supplied or generated by users remain subject to their applicable licenses.
